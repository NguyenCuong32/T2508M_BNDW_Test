The Post

The Post is a 2017 American political thriller film about The Washington Post and the publication of the Pentagon Papers. It was directed and produced by Steven Spielberg, and written by Liz Hannah and Josh Singer. It stars Meryl Streep as Katharine Graham, the publisher of The Washington Post, and Tom Hanks as Ben Bradlee, the longtime executive editor of The Washington Post, with Sarah Paulson, Bob Odenkirk, Tracy Letts, Bradley Whitford, David Cross, Bruce Greenwood, Carrie Coon, Alison Brie, and Matthew Rhys in supporting roles.

Set in 1971, The Post depicts the true story of attempts by journalists at The Washington Post to publish the infamous Pentagon Papers, a set of classified documents regarding the 20-year involvement of the United States government in the Vietnam War and earlier in French Indochina back to the 1940s.

The Dark Knight

The Dark Knight is a 2008 superhero crime thriller set in Gotham City, where Batman faces a rising wave of chaos orchestrated by the Joker. The film was directed and produced by Christopher Nolan and co-written with Jonathan Nolan from a story by Christopher Nolan and David S. Goyer. It stars Christian Bale as Bruce Wayne/Batman and Heath Ledger as the Joker, with Aaron Eckhart as Harvey Dent, alongside Michael Caine, Gary Oldman, Maggie Gyllenhaal, and Morgan Freeman in supporting roles.

Following the events of Batman Begins, Batman allies with Lieutenant James Gordon and District Attorney Harvey Dent to dismantle Gotham’s organized crime, but the Joker’s ruthless games force impossible moral choices, culminating in Dent’s tragic transformation into Two-Face. The film explores themes of heroism, sacrifice, and the fragile balance between security and freedom.
