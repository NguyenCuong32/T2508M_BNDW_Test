About Flakes

Flakes is a modern movie streaming platform built for film lovers. Our mission is to make watching your favorite movies easier, faster, and more enjoyable — anywhere, anytime.

Our Vision

We aim to create a personalized entertainment experience for everyone. With an expanding library and cutting-edge design, we're redefining how people discover and enjoy movies.

Our Journey

2022

Flakes was founded with the dream of revolutionizing online movie watching.

2023

We launched our beta platform and reached 10,000 users in just 3 months.

2024

Introduced AI-based recommendations and expanded to global audiences.

2025

Partnered with top studios and surpassed 1 million active users worldwide.
