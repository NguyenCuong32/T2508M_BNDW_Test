Featured: The Post

The Post is a 2017 American political thriller film about The Washington Post and the publication of the Pentagon Papers. The film was directed by Steven Spielberg and stars Meryl Streep as Katharine Graham, the first female publisher of a major American newspaper, and Tom Hanks as Ben Bradlee, the executive editor of the Post.

The film follows the true story of the 1971 Pentagon Papers controversy, when the New York Times and the Washington Post raced to publish a series of articles based on a top-secret government study of U.S. political and military involvement in Vietnam from 1945 to 1967.
